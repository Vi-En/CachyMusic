    Settings

    Privacy & network

    Local by design

    Your library, settings and playback remain on this device. No analytics, advertising or remote crash reports. Online metadata, artwork and lyrics searches are deliberate exceptions; only the selected query is sent to the provider.

    Listening history

    Qualified listening is recorded locally in this app profile. Background native playback may not be counted. Manage recording preferences or clear your history.

    Soulseek is optional

    Connecting to Soulseek discloses your IP address, account, search requests and selected shared files to the network. Share and download only material you have permission to distribute or obtain. Disconnect when finished.

    Hosted PWA

    Installation and browser-managed application updates may contact the host for application files. Local music and settings are not sent to that host. Use the packaged app or a local server for device-only operation.
